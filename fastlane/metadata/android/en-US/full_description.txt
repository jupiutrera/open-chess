Serious chess training, free and on your own device. No ads, no subscription and no account.

Open Chess runs Stockfish 19 on your phone, so the analysis, the bots and your data never leave it. It works offline.

<b>Game review</b>
Stockfish rates every move: brilliant, great, best, excellent, good, book, inaccuracy, mistake, miss or blunder. You get each player's accuracy, the evaluation graph and an explanation of what went wrong (forks, pins, trapped pieces, back-rank mates). Retry your mistakes until you find the right move, and export the review as a vertical video.

<b>13 bots with personality</b>
From a kid who just discovered the knight to an old-school master. Each one has favourite openings and talks to you during the game. Below 1400 they make human mistakes, not broken-engine ones. You can also make a clone bot that plays like any player, from their own games.

<b>17 challenges</b>
Stockfish with a silly rule: Meltfish loses 50 Elo every move, Growfish starts at 0 and climbs, Mirrorfish copies your moves, Panicfish gets weaker as its clock runs down…

<b>104,000 puzzles</b>
Lichess puzzles with a Glicko-2 rating, 47 themes, a daily puzzle, timed sprints, streaks, and puzzles made from your own mistakes. A weekly plan targets your weak spots.

<b>Openings</b>
An explorer with 3,865 named openings and a repertoire trainer with spaced repetition.

<b>Lessons</b>
Tactics, basic mates and endgames (opposition, Lucena, Philidor) that you practise against the engine.

<b>Also</b>
Live analysis with up to three engine lines, a position editor, FEN and PGN import and export, and game import from chess.com or Lichess by username. 9 piece sets, 5 boards, 5 sound sets, light and dark themes. In English, Spanish, French, Italian, Chinese and Japanese.

Open Chess is free software (GPL-3.0) and is not affiliated with chess.com or Lichess.
